Privacy Policy

At ZERONIC (zeronicpk.store), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy briefly outlines what data we collect, why we need it, and how we protect it when you browse our store or purchase wireless earbuds in Pakistan.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We only collect essential details needed to fulfill your orders and offer customer assistance:

  • Personal Details: Your full name, delivery address, active phone / WhatsApp number, and email address provided during checkout.
  • Order Details: Earbuds purchased, delivery preferences, invoice information, and payment choice (Cash on Delivery or Bank Transfer).
  • Device & Browsing Info: Non-personal technical data like browser type and device cookies to ensure fast and secure site navigation.
Payment Security: We do not store credit or debit card details. COD payments are collected directly upon parcel delivery.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used strictly for order fulfillment and support:

  • Processing, packing, and dispatching your earbud orders across Pakistan.
  • Sending SMS and WhatsApp shipping updates with real-time courier tracking numbers.
  • Managing our official 13-month replacement warranty and hassle-free returns.
  • Providing direct customer support via WhatsApp and helpline.

3. Information Sharing & Protection

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your delivery details (name, shipping address, and phone number) are shared exclusively with trusted courier partners (such as TCS, Leopards, and Trax) solely to deliver your package to your doorstep.

We employ industry-standard SSL encryption and protective protocols to ensure all customer records are safeguarded against unauthorized access.

4. Cookies & Your Choices

We use essential cookies solely to save items in your shopping bag and maintain smooth browsing. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time without restricting your access to view our catalog.

You have the right to request access, modification, or removal of your contact details from our records at any time by reaching out to our support team.
