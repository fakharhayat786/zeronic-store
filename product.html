Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, warranty claims, and features.

Q: What makes ZERONIC Flex Earbuds different from other earbuds?
A: Unlike generic earbuds, ZERONIC Flex Earbuds are software-integrated and connect to a dedicated companion app. This allows you to personalize custom 10-band EQ sound profiles, adjust Active Noise Cancellation depth, customize on-ear touch gestures, and receive over-the-air firmware upgrades.
Q: Do ZERONIC Flex Earbuds support ANC and Transparency Mode?
A: Yes! ZERONIC Flex features hardware-based Hybrid Active Noise Cancellation (up to -35dB) to isolate background traffic and office noise, as well as Transparency Mode to let external environmental sounds in when crossing roads or speaking to people.
Q: Is there a dedicated low-latency gaming mode?
A: Absolutely. With a simple tap on the earbud or via the app, you can switch on Low-Latency Game Mode (40ms response). This ensures gunshot sounds and footsteps in PUBG and COD Mobile are synchronized in real-time.
